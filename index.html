Derangiquarium
How To Play
🍽️
Feed your fish. Select the Food tool (bottom of the screen, or hotkey 1) and click in the water to drop a pellet. Fish swim over to eat it.
❗
Watch the hunger icons. A "!" means a fish is getting hungry and hunting for food; "!!" means it's getting desperate — leave it too long and it starves.
🧱
Platforms first. Every other building has to be placed touching a Platform (or resting on the very bottom of the tank) — build a Platform before anything else down there.
💨
Fans blow coins, Food, and Waste back up into the water column so your fish (and you) can reach them, instead of everything piling up out of sight.
🧲
Processors automatically pull in and bank any coin or Science Bubble that drifts into their intake side — no clicking required.
♻️
Auto-Feeders absorb Waste on one side and dispense fresh Food out the other, once they've processed enough loads.
💰
Click a coin to bank it yourself any time — Processors are a convenience, not a requirement. Waste can be left for a Suckerfish or an Auto-Feeder to clean up.
⛰️
The Mound (seabed, early game) cracks open for gold, unlocking new buildings and fish as you feed it money. It eventually shatters into...
🔬
...the Science Lab — the long-term goal. Spend Science Bubbles (dropped by the Science Octopus) and gold on its branching tree to unlock the Electric Eel, Suckerfish, Advanced buildings, and every Gene-Spliced hybrid fish.
🧤
The Merge tool (hotkey 3) lets you drag one Adult fish onto a matching one to combine them into a bigger, shinier fish, or drag an Adult Suckerfish/Eel/Octopus onto another Adult fish to splice a hybrid.
🏆
Tank Points (earned every time a fish grows to an adult) buy permanent upgrades in the Tank Upgrades panel — faster fish, better food, bigger coin caps, and more.
