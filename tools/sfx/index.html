Finsanity Dev Tools

How this works. Find sound files, drag them onto the matching card (or drop them into audio/sfx-candidates/<sound>/ and reload), click Play to audition, then Use this on the one you like. The pick is copied to audio/sfx/ and listed in audio/sfx/manifest.json with your gain setting.
  • Preview level defaults to 0.35, the game's default SFX level (50% slider x 0.7), so files and the current synth sound are compared at about the game's real loudness.
  • Each file shows its peak and RMS in dBFS. Use the card's gain slider to even out sounds that are much louder or quieter than the others.
  • Free sources: Kenney audio packs (CC0, no attribution), freesound.org (check each file's license; CC0 is easiest), Sonniss GDC bundles, Pixabay sound effects. Short files (under about 1.5 s) work best.
